The two files in this directory — rajdhani-700-subset.ttf and
rajdhani-600-subset.ttf — are subsets of Rajdhani Bold and Rajdhani SemiBold
made for `scripts/generate-app-screens.mjs` only: Latin letters, digits and the
punctuation a price needs. They are never served to a visitor; the app renders
its numbers in Rajdhani and its Cyrillic in the system face (Rajdhani has no
Cyrillic glyphs), and the marketing renders reproduce exactly that split.
